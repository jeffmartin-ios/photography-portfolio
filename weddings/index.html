Frequently Asked Questions

How do we book you for our wedding?

Booking is simple! Once you've chosen a collection, let's get in contact, and I'll send over a contract and an invoice for a 50% non-refundable retainer. Once those are signed and paid, your date is officially secured in my calendar.

When will we receive our photos?

You will receive your complete, professionally edited digital gallery within 4 to 8 weeks after your wedding date. I always aim to deliver a few sneak peeks within the first week!


If you opt for expedited delivery, you can expect your photos within 4 business days after your wedding.

What is your photography process during the wedding?

I take a hands off approach with gentle guidance towards you and your guests from time to time. I strive to capture candid moments. If you have specific ideas that you want me to try, we can also collaborate on taking your dream photo.

Can we print our own photos?

Absolutely. All of my wedding collections include a personal-use print release, which means you can download your high-resolution images and print them wherever you choose for yourself, family, and friends.
